### ⚜️ Philosophy & Background
Holding a **B.Sc. in Pure Mathematics & Computer Science (2010)**, combined with 13 years of leadership in the clinical/medical sector before dedicating myself fully to software, I treat frontend motion design, rendering states, and database models as cohesive mathematical systems. 

I construct robust, high-performance interfaces where motion is driven by **physical/numerical models and custom shaders** rather than canned easing templates.
